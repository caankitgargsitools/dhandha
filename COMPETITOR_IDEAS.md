## Standout ideas (to beat all of them, not just match)

1. **Bid-to-win loop**: award data + cost planner + QCBS advice + rival intelligence → "rival X quoted 8% below estimate in 4 of their last 5 Bihar audits; your best winning price is ₹Y". TenderTiger sells the pieces separately.
2. **One WhatsApp brain**: tender alerts, the tender assistant, lead replies, AI-call follow-ups and booking links in one WhatsApp, in Hindi/Hinglish.
3. **Compliance-first AI calling for India** at a flat price per minute below Bolna (about ₹5.5/min self-serve) — our own voice server has no platform fee.
4. **"Never type twice" across the suite**: the Vault fills GeM registration, IndiaMART profile, quotes and bid formats; sell a done-for-you GeM / tender desk on top.
5. **Credits that follow results**: pay-per-tender entry, a free tier, and "cost per win" so a business sees rupees in and rupees won.
